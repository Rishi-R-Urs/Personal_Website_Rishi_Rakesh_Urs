About Me

My name is Rishi Rakesh Urs, and I am currently a Data Science and Business Administration student at Northeastern University concentrating in FinTech, with minors in Economics and Interdisciplinary Artificial Intelligence.

Why data

Coming from a family that specializes in the education industry, I gained early exposure to business and developed a strong appreciation for learning. My interest in data analytics stems from the joy of discovery and the culture of continuous learning and adaptability the field demands, especially with the constant evolution of new technologies. One such technology is Artificial Intelligence, which has become a pivotal tool in modern data analytics and business.

AI will not replace your job; however, the person who knows how to use AI better than you will.One of my professors

In practice

I am experienced in applying data-driven approaches to solve business problems through professional and academic experiences. For my first co-op, I worked as a Marketing and Sentiment Feedback Program Specialist at Northeastern University. Here, I applied my learning by working through the entire data lifecycle: conversing with more than 3,000 students to collect feedback, analyzing that feedback to generate actionable insights, effectively communicating those insights to non technical stakeholders, and implementing student-facing initiatives.

I really started to embrace data analytics after seeing the positive impact it had on the student experience. I heard students' positive feedback firsthand about the changes that were made and saw those improvements reflected in higher CSAT scores. What made me most proud was knowing that the data insights I produced and the student-focused initiatives I helped with contributed to a 194.6% increase in Community Meal Plan sales.

Beyond the classroom

Shifting focus into my academic experiences, in my freshman year I participated in the Northeastern Global Scholars Program, studying in London and Oakland, where I developed adaptability and gained exposure to vast cultures, food, and ways of life.

While my experiences have shaped who I am today, I believe they are only the beginning of a lifelong journey of learning.

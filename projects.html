Mathematical Model Representing the Impact of Economic Factors on Commodities and Rates

Sep 2022 – May 2023

Associated with Canadian International School

The intent of this project was to create statistical models and calculate the correlations and significance among various economic factors and commodities. The project compared and contrasted the changes in the dollar with the changes in the price of gold and determined whether the repo rate had a greater influence on both. Additionally, it analyzed how interest rates influenced inflation rates. Historical data on gold prices, the dollar-to-rupee exchange rate, inflation rates, and repo rates was collected from secondary sources. The data was used as both a predictor variable and a response variable for statistical analysis. Pearson's Correlation formula was used to calculate the correlation coefficient. A t-test was applied to verify the significance of dependency between the variables using hypothesis testing. The data was graphed using scatter plots to identify patterns. Using MS Excel, linear and polynomial regression trend lines provided a visual representation of the correlation between the variables, along with equations for the lines of best fit. The results helped identify favorable investment opportunities and appropriate timing to maximize returns.
